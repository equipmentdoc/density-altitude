Calculated using an ISA atmosphere density model. Without dew point, dry air is assumed. METAR readings are observations, not forecasts.

Calculation details & limitations

Station pressure is estimated from the reported altimeter setting and airport elevation using the standard altimeter relationship. Density altitude is determined by matching computed air density to the ISA atmosphere.

Pressure altitude and density altitude are rounded to the nearest foot. Published airport elevation may differ from the actual runway threshold elevation. A live weather fetch requires connectivity and a responding AviationWeather.gov endpoint; if it fails, previously entered values remain available.

Use official weather and the aircraft POH/AFM for takeoff, landing, and climb planning. This calculator is supplemental and is not an approved flight-planning source.
